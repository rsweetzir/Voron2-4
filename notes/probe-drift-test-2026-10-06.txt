Findings
- The bed reaches 55C in about 1 min, but the probed height keeps moving for the whole 25 min.
  It jumps +0.095 mm in the first 2 min, falls quickly until about 8 min, then creeps down at
  about 0.0025 mm/min. Total swing is 0.115 mm, over half a 0.2 mm first layer.
- Before this change PRINT_START had no soak below 80C, so QGL, Z homing and the mesh ran in
  the first 1-3 min, near the peak. That alone can put the first layer 0.06-0.1 mm off.
- After a 10 min soak the remaining drift is about 0.03-0.05 mm over the next 15 min, and the
  Z re-home after the soak cancels the offset at print start. PLA_SOAK set to 10.
- First sample after each 60 s dwell at Z10 reads low: about 0.03-0.06 mm early in the test,
  0.06-0.10 mm after 17 min. The tolerance retry rejects it (samples_tolerance 0.015), so
  results stay good, but a sample is wasted each time. Likely the probe warming while it waits
  above the hot bed. Watch it in meshes; a throwaway first sample would fix it.
